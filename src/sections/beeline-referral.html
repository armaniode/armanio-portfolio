The referral program connects
user benefit with Beeline’s loyalty ecosystem: users invite friends or family members and receive points that can later be converted into rubles. For the business, it creates  a digital growth channel that can promote Beeline products beyond external advertising

We designed the mechanic as
a reusable referral model not only
for SIM-card acquisition, but also for additional products. Metrics collection is still in progress; the key success indicators  are referral engagement, invite-to-activation conversion and acquisition cost
