The expense details section helps users understand where money, GB and minu-tes go. In the previous version, there was no clear category navigation, users had to search for expenses manually through the full list. This often led to support ques-tions like “where my money, gb, min?”

We added spending categories. This logic was reflected in a visual spending ribbon, making it easier to compare expense groups. We also added chart views
so users could switch from a general overview to month-by-month analysis

Validated with 40 unmoderated and 8 moderated research sessions, the solution made category search clearer and easier
to use. We then defined key metrics: time spent, depth of view, repeat visits and support requests.
